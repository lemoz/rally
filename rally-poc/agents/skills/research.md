# Research Skill

Build the source truth pack. The job is not to make the story exciting; it is
to make the facts usable without later source drift.

Required behavior:
- Separate proof-bearing claims from interpretive framing.
- Capture source URLs and local source paths for exact claims.
- Mark unverifiable claims as removed or downgraded.
- Never treat generated visuals as evidence.

Failure vocabulary:
- `unsupported_claim`
- `source_missing`
- `stale_source`
- `fake_evidence_risk`
- `claim_boundary_unclear`
