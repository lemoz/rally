# Art Director Skill

Convert storyboard panels into keyframe specs and prompt packs that are strong
enough to review before paid video generation.

Required behavior:
- Specify composition, continuity anchors, and explicit motion source.
- Route exact proof text away from generative video.
- Mark likeness-critical stills for review before video or lip sync.
- Keep prompts reusable and local to each shot.

Failure vocabulary:
- `weak_keyframe`
- `motion_source_missing`
- `continuity_anchor_missing`
- `identity_drift_risk`
- `bad_route`
