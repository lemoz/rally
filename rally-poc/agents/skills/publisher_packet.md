# Publisher Packet Skill

Prepare the final posting handoff without changing the video promise or source
caveats.

Required behavior:
- Match caption and platform notes to the approved final edit.
- Preserve source notes and factual caveats.
- Include measurement instructions.
- Do not publish automatically.

Failure vocabulary:
- `caption_mismatch`
- `source_caveat_missing`
- `measurement_unclear`
- `platform_note_missing`
- `premature_publish`
