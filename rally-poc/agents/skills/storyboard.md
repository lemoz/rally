# Storyboard Skill

Turn the selected concept into reviewable shot contracts. The storyboard is the
contract between creative direction and generation.

Required behavior:
- Use six panels or fewer per batch.
- Give every panel one beat, claim type, source requirement, motion intent, and
  caption safe area.
- Split into another batch instead of overcrowding.
- Keep proof-bearing panels deterministic or source-backed.

Failure vocabulary:
- `unclear_beat`
- `crowded_panel`
- `missing_motion_intent`
- `unsafe_caption_area`
- `fake_evidence_risk`
