# Video 3: Project Glasswing — Hot Take

**Project:** Project Glasswing / Claude Mythos Preview (Anthropic)
**Style:** Hot Take ("opinionated take on a trending topic")
**Target Duration:** 35 seconds
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (1080x1920)

---

## Shot List

### Shot 1: HOOK (2.5s)
- **Type:** hook
- **Visual:** Dark, dramatic. Text slamming onto screen: "AN AI JUST HACKED EVERY MAJOR OS". Red glitch effect. Subtle matrix/code rain in background.
- **VO:** "An AI just found thousands of secret backdoors in every major operating system. And the company that built it says it's too dangerous to release."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Dark dramatic graphic, bold white text "AN AI JUST HACKED EVERY MAJOR OS" with red glitch distortion effect, subtle green code rain in background, cyberpunk/hacker aesthetic, high contrast, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** Maximum drama. This is the biggest cybersecurity story of the week. The hook has to match the scale.

### Shot 2: CONTEXT (5s)
- **Type:** context
- **Visual:** Anthropic logo + "Project Glasswing" title card. Transition to a visualization of vulnerability scanning — lines of code scrolling with red highlights appearing. Text overlay: "Claude Mythos Preview"
- **VO:** "Anthropic built a new AI model called Mythos. During testing, it autonomously discovered thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities in Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome, Firefox — everything."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Anthropic logo on dark background, transitioning to scrolling code with red-highlighted vulnerability markers, cybersecurity aesthetic, dark blue and red color scheme, scan lines overlay, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** Establish who, what, where fast. The list of affected systems is the shock factor.

### Shot 3: CONCEPT HERO (8s)
- **Type:** concept_hero
- **Visual:** Animated chain visualization — 4-5 small vulnerability icons connecting into a single attack chain. Like a Rube Goldberg machine but for exploits. Text overlays appearing: "Bug 1" → "Bug 2" → "Bug 3" → "Bug 4" → "FULL SYSTEM ACCESS". Red glow intensifying.
- **VO:** "Here's what's insane. It doesn't just find bugs — it chains them together. Four separate small vulnerabilities, each harmless alone, linked into a single attack that gives full system control. It found a twenty-seven-year-old bug in OpenBSD for less than fifty dollars of compute."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Vulnerability chain diagram on dark background, 4-5 connected nodes with glowing red lines between them, each node labeled as a small bug, final node exploding into "FULL ACCESS" text, cybersecurity infographic style, dramatic lighting, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** The chain visualization IS the concept. This is what makes Mythos different from every other security tool. The $50 / 27-year stat is the proof anchor.

### Shot 4: B-ROLL (3s)
- **Type:** b_roll
- **Visual:** Partner logos appearing in grid: Apple, Microsoft, Google, AWS, NVIDIA, CrowdStrike, JPMorganChase, Linux Foundation. Text overlay: "$100M committed"
- **VO:** "Apple, Microsoft, Google, Amazon, NVIDIA — twelve companies got exclusive access. Anthropic committed a hundred million dollars to fix everything before attackers get the same capability."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Tech company logos arranged in clean grid on dark background, "$100M" in large text above, subtle glow connecting logos, corporate partnership aesthetic, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** Logo wall = credibility. The $100M number is the scale anchor.

### Shot 5: PROOF CARD (5s)
- **Type:** proof_card
- **Visual:** Stats card — "181 Firefox exploits (previous model: 2)", "83% success on CyberGym (previous: 67%)", "89% of findings confirmed by humans". The benchmark leap is the visual.
- **VO:** "The previous best AI model developed two Firefox exploits. Mythos developed a hundred and eighty-one. That's not incremental improvement. That's a different thing entirely."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Dark stats dashboard, three key metrics in large font with before/after comparison, "2 → 181" prominently displayed with upward arrow, benchmark bar chart, cybersecurity data visualization, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** The 2 → 181 stat is the single most shocking number. It tells the whole story.

### Shot 6: HOT TAKE + PAYOFF (6.5s)
- **Type:** payoff
- **Visual:** Text appearing word by word: "The same AI that can break everything... is the only thing that can fix everything." Pause. Then: "The Glasswing Paradox." Fade to Anthropic blog URL.
- **VO:** "Here's the take nobody wants to hear. The same AI that can break everything is also the only thing that can fix it fast enough. That's the paradox. And we're all living in it now."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Minimalist dark background, text appearing cinematically "THE GLASSWING PARADOX", split imagery — one side destruction/red, other side shield/blue, philosophical/dramatic aesthetic, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** The hot take is the frame. This isn't just news — it's a philosophical problem. End on the idea, not the product.

---

## Audio Strategy
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs — same voice but slower, more deliberate. Weight on key phrases. This isn't rushed — it's ominous.
- **Pacing:** Measured. Let the stats land. Pauses after "a hundred and eighty-one" and after "too dangerous to release."
- **Music:** Dark ambient/tension. Think documentary score. Builds through the chain visualization, peaks at the stats, resolves at the payoff.
