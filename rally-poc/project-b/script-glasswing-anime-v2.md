# Glasswing: The Anime — Story-Driven Script

**Style:** PsyopAnime — character-driven, dialogue-based, dramatic anime
**Target Duration:** 45-60 seconds
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 with cinematic letterbox bars (black bars top/bottom for widescreen anime feel)
**Audio:** Dramatic music bed + character dialogue via ElevenLabs (multiple voices or dramatic narrator quoting characters)

---

## Characters

| Character | Anime Design | Voice |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Dario** (Anthropic CEO) | Dark suit, sharp features, glasses reflecting code, calm but intense | Deep, measured, authoritative |
| **Mythos** (the AI) | Not humanoid — a glowing red eye/entity behind screens, code flowing around it like tendrils | No voice — shown through action |
| **The Researcher** | Young woman, lab coat, exhausted eyes, monitors reflecting in her face | Urgent, slightly afraid |
| **The General** (composite military/tech exec) | Graying hair, stern jaw, military/corporate uniform | Gruff, skeptical |

## Scene Breakdown

### SCENE 1: THE DISCOVERY (8s)
**[Interior — Anthropic research lab, night. Banks of monitors. A lone researcher stares at her screen.]**

- **Shot 1a (3s):** Wide shot of dark lab, blue monitor light illuminating the researcher from behind. She's leaning forward, hand over her mouth.
- **Shot 1b (2.5s):** Close-up on her eyes. They're wide. The monitors reflect scrolling red code in her pupils.
- **Shot 1c (2.5s):** Her POV — the screen shows cascading vulnerability reports, numbers climbing: 47... 218... 1,043... The counter keeps going.

**DIALOGUE (subtitle):**
> "It found over a thousand zero-days. In one night."

---

### SCENE 2: THE CHAIN (10s)
**[The AI at work. Abstract visualization of Mythos operating.]**

- **Shot 2a (4s):** A massive red eye opens in darkness — Mythos activating. Code streams orbit around it like a solar system. Camera slowly pushes in.
- **Shot 2b (3s):** Rapid montage — four vulnerability nodes light up in sequence. Each one cracks open like glass breaking. Red energy arcs between them forming a chain.
- **Shot 2c (3s):** The chain completes — all four vulnerabilities connect. A door swings open at the end. Text appears: "FULL SYSTEM ACCESS." The red eye watches.

**DIALOGUE (subtitle):**
> "It doesn't just find the cracks. It threads them together. Four flaws no one would notice... become one key that opens everything."

---

### SCENE 3: THE BOARDROOM (12s)
**[Interior — Anthropic headquarters. Dark wood, dramatic shadows. Dario stands at the head of a long table.]**

- **Shot 3a (3s):** Wide shot of the boardroom. Dario stands, silhouetted against a massive screen showing the vulnerability count. His reflection is visible in the polished table.
- **Shot 3b (3s):** Medium close-up on Dario. Heavy shadows across his face. He removes his glasses slowly.
- **Shot 3c (3s):** Cut to the General, sitting across the table, arms crossed. Skeptical expression. Behind him, a window shows the city at night.
- **Shot 3d (3s):** Back to Dario, extreme close-up on his eyes. Dead serious.

**DIALOGUE (subtitles):**
> DARIO: "We built something that can break every system on the planet."
>
> GENERAL: "Then we use it. Before someone else builds one."
>
> DARIO: "You don't understand. We ARE someone else. Every lab in the world is six months behind us."

---

### SCENE 4: THE PROOF (8s)
**[Quick cuts — the scale of what Mythos found.]**

- **Shot 4a (2s):** Anime-style montage: Windows logo shatters. macOS apple cracks. Linux penguin glitches. Chrome icon fractures. Each one breaks in dramatic anime style with speed lines.
- **Shot 4b (3s):** The researcher pulls up the benchmark screen. Dramatic zoom on the numbers: "Previous AI: 2 exploits. Mythos: 181." The 181 pulses red.
- **Shot 4c (3s):** The General leans forward, his expression changed. No longer skeptical. Now afraid.

**DIALOGUE (subtitle):**
> "The last model found two exploits in Firefox. Mythos found a hundred and eighty-one. This isn't evolution. This is a different species."

---

### SCENE 5: THE DECISION (10s)
**[The weight of what to do.]**

- **Shot 5a (3s):** Dario walks to the window. City lights below. His reflection stares back at him.
- **Shot 5b (2s):** Flash cut — a montage of company logos appearing one by one in holographic panels: Apple, Microsoft, Google, NVIDIA. Twelve total.
- **Shot 5c (2.5s):** Dario turns back to the room. Camera pushes in slowly.
- **Shot 5d (2.5s):** Extreme close-up on Dario's eyes. Determination.

**DIALOGUE (subtitles):**
> DARIO: "We give it to twelve companies. Quietly. A hundred million dollars to patch everything it found."
>
> GENERAL: "And if someone else builds one before you're done?"
>
> DARIO: "Then God help us all."

---

### SCENE 6: THE PARADOX — CLOSE (7s)
**[Final beat. The philosophical weight.]**

- **Shot 6a (3s):** Slow zoom out from Mythos' red eye. The eye is watching everything — screens, code, the world.
- **Shot 6b (4s):** Final wide shot — the researcher stands alone in the dark lab. Behind her, Mythos' eye glows on the main screen. She's staring at it. It's staring back. Title card fades in: "PROJECT GLASSWING"

**DIALOGUE (subtitle):**
> "The thing that can destroy everything... is the only thing that can save it."
>
> "PROJECT GLASSWING"

## Production Notes

### Visual Style (per PsyopAnime reference)
- **90s anime aesthetic**: Heavy cel-shading, Akira/Ghost in the Shell lighting
- **Extreme close-ups on eyes** for every dramatic beat — this is the signature
- **Dark, shadow-heavy lighting** — characters half-lit, dramatic contrast
- **Letterbox bars** (top/bottom black bars even in 9:16 — signals "cinematic anime")
- **Speed lines and impact frames** for action beats
- **Reflections** — code reflecting in glasses, monitors reflecting in eyes, city reflecting in windows

### Audio Strategy
**Option A (PsyopAnime style):** No voiceover. Dramatic instrumental music only. All dialogue delivered via yellow subtitles on black bar. This is how PsyopAnime does it.

**Option B (TikTok optimized):** Dramatic narrator voiceover reading the dialogue lines. Different vocal tone for each character (ElevenLabs can do this with different voice settings on the same voice, or use different voices).

**Recommendation:** Option A for the anime purist version. Option B for TikTok reach (85% watch muted, but the 15% who listen will be more engaged with voice).

### Shot Generation Approach
- Every shot is T2V (Seedance 2.0) — no infographics, no NB2 stills
- Prompts must specify: "90s anime cel-shaded style, dark dramatic lighting, cinematic composition"
- Character consistency: describe each character the same way in every shot they appear
- Extreme close-ups: "extreme close-up on eyes, anime style, dramatic shadows across face"
- The 181 stat is the only text that appears in-frame (Shot 4b) — generate as NB2 anime still

### Estimated Shots: 15
- 14 T2V clips via Seedance 2.0 (various durations 2-4s each)
- 1 NB2 still for the 181 stat (anime style)
