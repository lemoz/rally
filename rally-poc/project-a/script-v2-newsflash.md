# Video 2: OpenScreen — News Flash

**Project:** OpenScreen (free Screen Studio alternative)
**Style:** News Flash ("breaking: X just launched/happened")
**Target Duration:** 22 seconds
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (1080x1920)

---

## Shot List

### Shot 1: HOOK (2s)
- **Type:** hook
- **Visual:** Urgent/breaking news style graphic. Red "BREAKING" banner. Text: "A solo dev just killed Screen Studio's business model". Dark background, news ticker aesthetic.
- **VO:** "A solo developer just killed Screen Studio's entire business model."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Breaking news graphic, red BREAKING banner at top, bold white headline text on dark background, news broadcast aesthetic, lens flare accent, urgent feel, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** News flash style = urgency. The claim is bold enough to stop scrolling.

### Shot 2: PROOF CARD (4s)
- **Type:** proof_card
- **Visual:** GitHub trending page showing OpenScreen at #1. Star count prominently displayed. Text overlay: "26,000+ stars in one week". Yellow highlight on the star count.
- **VO:** "OpenScreen hit twenty-six thousand GitHub stars in a single week. It's the fastest trending repo right now."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** N/A — use real GitHub screenshot or stylized version
- **Notes:** Proof first. In news flash style, you lead with evidence, not explanation.

### Shot 3: CONCEPT HERO (7s)
- **Type:** concept_hero
- **Visual:** OpenScreen editor UI in action. Quick cuts showing: (1) recording toolbar appearing, (2) auto-zoom on a click, (3) motion blur on a pan, (4) export button. Text overlays: "Auto-zoom" → "Motion blur" → "Free forever"
- **VO:** "Auto-zoom on every click. Motion blur built in. Webcam overlay. Timeline editing. Everything Screen Studio charges three hundred and fifty a year for. Free. Open source. MIT licensed."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Stylized screen recording interface with zoom effect, dark theme, motion blur trails, feature labels appearing as floating badges, tech product showcase style, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** Rapid feature dump. News flash style packs info dense. Every sentence is a headline.

### Shot 4: EVIDENCE (5s)
- **Type:** evidence
- **Visual:** Three-column comparison. "Screen Studio: $29/mo, Mac only" vs "OpenScreen: $0, Mac+Win+Linux". The creator's README quote: "I'm new to open source, idk what I'm doing lol" — with 26k stars next to it.
- **VO:** "Screen Studio: twenty-nine a month, Mac only. OpenScreen: free, runs everywhere. Built by someone who says quote I'm new to open source, I don't know what I'm doing."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Comparison card, dark background, two columns with price comparison, quote text at bottom in handwritten style font, contrast between corporate pricing and indie developer energy, 9:16 vertical
- **Notes:** The README quote is the emotional hook. Underdog energy. This is what makes people share.

### Shot 5: PAYOFF (4s)
- **Type:** payoff
- **Visual:** OpenScreen logo. "github.com/siddharthvaddem/openscreen". Text: "Link in bio". Simple, clean.
- **VO:** "Link in bio. Go star it before they figure out how to charge for this too."
- **FLUX prompt direction:** Minimal dark card, centered logo, URL, clean
- **Notes:** The closing joke lands the tone — slightly irreverent, pro-open-source.

- **Music:** Slightly more intense instrumental bed. Tension/urgency feel.
