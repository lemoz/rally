# Video 2: OpenScreen — News Flash (v2 - mixed media)

**Project:** OpenScreen
**Style:** News Flash
**Target Duration:** ~40 seconds
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (1080x1920)

---

## Shot List

### Shot 1: HOOK (4s)
- **Asset type:** TEXT-TO-VIDEO
- **Prompt:** "Breaking news broadcast studio, red BREAKING NEWS banner flashes on screen, camera pushes into a holographic display, dramatic red lighting, urgent news environment, professional broadcast set"
- **VO:** "A solo developer just killed Screen Studio's entire business model."
- **Why T2V:** A living, breathing news studio sells the urgency far better than a static graphic.

### Shot 2: PROOF (6s)
- **Asset type:** IMAGE-TO-VIDEO (animate NB2 image)
- **NB2 prompt:** "GitHub Trending page dark theme. OpenScreen at #1 with TRENDING badge. Star count 26.1k highlighted in yellow circle. Description: Free open-source screen recorder with auto-zoom and motion blur. TypeScript."
- **Video prompt:** "The star count number ticks upward, the trending badge pulses, a yellow highlight circle draws around the star count, subtle scroll animation"
- **VO:** "OpenScreen hit twenty-six thousand GitHub stars in a single week. It's the fastest trending repo right now."
- **Why I2V:** The star count ticking up is viscerally satisfying. Static GitHub page is boring.

### Shot 3: CONCEPT HERO (16s)
- **Asset type:** TEXT-TO-VIDEO
- **Prompt:** "Close-up of a computer screen showing a professional screen recording application in action, the view auto-zooms smoothly into a clicked area with motion blur trails, then the user clicks Export and a progress bar fills, dark theme UI, cinematic shallow depth of field"
- **VO:** "Auto-zoom on every click. Motion blur built in. Webcam overlay. Timeline editing. Everything Screen Studio charges three hundred and fifty a year for. Free. Open source. MIT licensed."
- **Why T2V:** Feature showcase needs to feel like a real demo, not a slideshow.

### Shot 4: EVIDENCE (11s)
- **Asset type:** IMAGE-TO-VIDEO (animate NB2 image)
- **NB2 prompt:** "Dark comparison card. Top: Screen Studio $29/month Mac Only vs OpenScreen FREE All Platforms. Bottom: quote in italic handwritten font: I am new to open source, idk what I am doing lol with GitHub avatar and 26,100 stars."
- **Video prompt:** "The price comparison slides in from left, then the quote fades in below with a gentle typewriter effect, the star count glows"
- **VO:** "Screen Studio: twenty-nine a month, Mac only. OpenScreen: free, runs everywhere. Built by someone who says quote I'm new to open source, I don't know what I'm doing."
- **Why I2V:** The quote reveal needs dramatic timing. Animation lets the punchline land.

### Shot 5: PAYOFF (4s)
- **Asset type:** IMAGE-TO-VIDEO (animate NB2 image)
- **NB2 prompt:** "Minimalist dark background, OpenScreen logo with blue glow, github.com/openscreen text below"
- **Video prompt:** "Logo pulses with soft blue glow, subtle particles drift upward"
- **VO:** "Link in bio. Go star it before they figure out how to charge for this too."
- **Why I2V:** Same polished closer as V1.
